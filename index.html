Our Story

Where Parrots Find
Their Paradise

Nestled in the heart of Key West, Nancy Forrester's Secret Garden is a magical oasis where rescued parrots live in a lush tropical paradise. Founded by artist and environmental activist Nancy Forrester, our sanctuary has been providing a loving home for orphaned and abandoned parrots for over three decades.

As one of the first parrot sanctuaries in the United States, we're dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming these magnificent birds while educating visitors about the importance of parrot conservation.

Our Mission

"Adopt — Do Not Shop"

For over three decades, Nancy Forrester's Secret Garden has been at the forefront of parrot rescue and education. As one of the smallest yet most dedicated sanctuaries in the United States, we've made it our life's work to give abandoned, abused, and orphaned parrots a second chance at happiness.

Every year, thousands of parrots are abandoned by owners who underestimate the 50+ year commitment these intelligent birds require. Many suffer from neglect, improper care, or abuse. We believe every parrot deserves love, proper nutrition, enrichment, and a forever home.

🛡️

Rescue

We take in parrots from all backgrounds — surrendered pets, abuse survivors, and birds in need of specialized care.

💚

Rehabilitate

Each bird receives individualized attention, proper nutrition, veterinary care, and the patience needed to heal.

🎓

Educate

Through daily "Parrot 101" sessions, we teach visitors about responsible parrot ownership and conservation.

🏠

Rehome

When possible, we find qualified forever homes for birds ready for adoption, ensuring they never suffer again.

Together, we can stop parrot abuse and give these beautiful birds the life they deserve.

Our Feathered Family

Meet the Parrots

Home to eight different species of macaws and two hybrids, each with their own unique personality and story.

Scarlet Macaws

Vibrant red, yellow, and blue beauties known for their intelligence and playful nature.

Blue & Gold Macaws

Stunning blue and yellow feathers with charming personalities that love to interact.

Military Macaws

Magnificent green macaws with striking red foreheads and gentle dispositions.

Hybrid Macaws

Unique crossbreeds displaying spectacular color combinations found nowhere else.

Every parrot has a story. Come meet them in person and hear their tales!
